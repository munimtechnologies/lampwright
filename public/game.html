How to play

▶
  1. The lamp shines one beam of light across the board.
  2. Tap an empty square to place a mirror. Tap again to flip it, a third time to remove it.
  3. Bend the beam through every crystal. Walls stop it, crystals let it pass, grey mirrors are fixed.
  4. Use as few mirrors as you can. The fewest possible is par; you get par + 1.
  5. Lock in to save your result. Most days have several par routes: can everyone here find them all?

A new puzzle is posted every day at 00:05 UTC.

Keyboard: Tab to the board, arrow keys to move, Enter or Space to place or flip a mirror.
